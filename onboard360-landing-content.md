## Core Features

**Section eyebrow:** What's inside
**Section heading:** Three systems. One onboarding experience.
**Section subheading:** Onboard360 isn't a form builder with extra steps. It's three purpose-built systems working together.

### Feature 1 — Workflow Builder
**Title:** Checklists that know who they're for
**Description:** Build onboarding paths once, per role — engineering, sales, support, whatever your org looks like. Assign it, and every new hire gets exactly the steps that apply to them. Nothing generic, nothing missed.

### Feature 2 — Document Pipeline
**Title:** Paperwork that files itself
**Description:** Upload it once. Onboard360's document pipeline routes, tags, and stores every policy, form, and handbook — so nothing lives in someone's downloads folder, and nothing gets handed to a new hire twice.

### Feature 3 — Qorra, the onboarding assistant
**Title:** An assistant that's actually read the handbook
**Description:** Qorra is grounded in your company's own documents — policies, guides, FAQs. New hires ask a question, Qorra answers from what your company actually wrote, not a generic script.

## How It Works

**Section eyebrow:** The flow
**Section heading:** From day zero to day one, automatically

1. **HR builds the path.** Pick a role, assemble the checklist once. Onboard360 remembers it.
2. **A new hire is assigned.** The right tasks, documents, and mentors are attached automatically — no manual setup per person.
3. **Qorra fills the gaps.** Questions that would normally interrupt HR get answered instantly, grounded in your real documents.
4. **Progress is visible.** Managers and mentors see exactly where every new hire stands, without asking.
